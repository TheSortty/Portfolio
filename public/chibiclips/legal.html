ChibiClips Terms and Privacy

ChibiClips makes short animated stories where the audience decides what happens next by voting in the comments. These terms and this policy cover the ChibiClips app, an internal tool that connects only to our own TikTok accounts.

Terms of Service

1Who we are

ChibiClips is an independent animation channel that publishes interactive stories (such as PORTERO, SEGUNDA VIDA and NIVEL) on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, in English and Spanish.

2What the app does

The ChibiClips app is used only by the ChibiClips team, with the ChibiClips TikTok accounts (English and Spanish). It does three things:

  • signs in to our own accounts with TikTok Login Kit;
  • reads basic profile information, follower counts and the statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to prepare a performance report;
  • uploads our own finished episodes to our own accounts as drafts, which we review and publish inside the TikTok app.

The app is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.

3Acceptable use

The app may be used only with accounts owned by ChibiClips and only in line with TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms.

4Content

All episodes uploaded through the app are original work owned by ChibiClips. Comments and votes posted by viewers on TikTok remain subject to TikTok's own terms.

5No warranty

The app is provided as is, for internal use. We may change or stop it at any time.

6Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

1Data we access

Only data from the ChibiClips accounts that sign in to the app:

  • basic profile information (display name, avatar, account identifier) through user.info.basic;
  • account statistics (followers, following, likes, video count) through user.info.stats;
  • our own videos and their public counts (views, likes, comments, shares) through video.list.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.

2How we use it

Only to prepare an internal report, twice a day, that tells our team how each episode is performing, and to decide which story continues. We do not sell, rent or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on our own server, readable only by the account that runs the report, and are never published. Reports are kept in our private storage.

4Retention and deletion

We keep statistics as long as they are useful for the report. Disconnecting the app from the account in TikTok's settings stops all access; we then delete the stored tokens.

5Children

The app is not directed to children and processes no data from minors.
